## Rules that apply to every bay

**Cube budget.** The physical rig has **two cubes per colour**. A cell holds one colour
at level 1 (1 cube) or level 2 (2 cubes) — never two colours in one cell. So a bay may
ask for at most 2 of a colour: one L2 cell, or two L1 cells, or one L1 cell. Anything
more is unsolvable on the real table. Three-high stacks do not exist.

**Safe footing.** The blocks that solve an obstacle are also the player's footing.
Nothing may kill the player while they're standing on the thing that solved it.

**Crates are indestructible.** The real cube is still on the operator's table and the
game must mirror it, so no obstacle may destroy a crate. Resolve contact some other way
(bay 1 slows or jams the wall instead).

**One obstacle per bay.** Each 4×3 bay is a single obstacle, not one-block-one-trap.

---

## Two ways a bay can be solved

1. **Placement** (bays 2–9, the default) — `checkSolved` watches `BlockSync.CellsChanged`
   and marks the bay solved when every `solveCell` holds a crate of the right colour at
   or above the required level.

2. **Action** (`solveMode = "switches"`, bay 1) — the obstacle owns `state.solved` and
   sets it itself; `checkSolved` skips the bay entirely and `solveCells` degrades to a
   "stack here" hint. Use this whenever the crates are the *means* rather than the goal.

Both routes go through one `state.setSolved`, which owns the label and the edge-triggered
`onSolved`. A switch-mode bay **cannot be un-solved by clearing cells** — the R hotkey
resets every bay instead.

## Known gaps across the whole project

- **`ActiveBayTracker` doesn't exist.** `DebugMode` hardcodes Bay 1, so only Bay 1 is
  wired to the arm. Everything else is built but unreachable by the real flow.
- **No `GameState` / checkpoints.** Dying respawns you at world spawn.
- **No run clock, scoring or malfunctions** (plan Sections 6 and 9) — `Config` has the
  placeholder values, nothing reads them.
- **Warehouse geometry isn't reproducible from source.** It was generated by command-bar
  scripts that weren't kept; it now exists only in the `.rbxl`. Editing layout values in
  `Config.lua` will **not** move the baked bays.
- **Two-player support** undecided.
- **Reset is partial.** Obstacles that show "solved" as a one-shot tween don't animate
  back; their loops resume but the visual stays cleared.

## Testing notes that cost time to learn

- `execute_luau`'s **Server datamodel is a separate Luau VM** with its own module cache.
  `require(BlockSync)` there returns a *different instance* than the running game, so it
  proves nothing. Drive tests through the real `CellEdit` / `DebugHotkey` remotes.
- **Teleporting a test character can skip the thing you're testing.** A walk-in path has
  to be exercised, or checked geometrically (raycast / `GetPartBoundsInBox`).
- **`Humanoid:MoveTo` from the server does nothing** for a player-controlled character.
- **`Health = MaxHealth` does not revive a destroyed Humanoid.** Use `pl:LoadCharacter()`.
- Mirror edits back out of Studio and verify with a checksum; transcription drifts
  silently otherwise.
